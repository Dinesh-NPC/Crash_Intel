Time Pattern Insight
The evening rush hour (5–7 PM) consistently records the highest accident count, driven by peak traffic volume and driver fatigue after work. Friday sees the most accidents of any day — likely due to weekend travel combined with end-of-week fatigue. Road authorities should prioritize enforcement and patrol during these windows.

Environmental Insight
Clear weather paradoxically records the most accidents — not because it is dangerous, but because more people drive in good conditions. However, rain, fog, and ice dramatically increase severity even when fewer accidents occur. Wet and icy roads reduce tyre grip and double stopping distances, making speed control critical in adverse weather.
