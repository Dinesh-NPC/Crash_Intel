⚠️ About This Model
Fatal crashes are very rare (<0.2% of all accidents). To handle this extreme class imbalance, the training set was oversampled — fatal cases were duplicated to ~33% of training data. The model uses class_weight='balanced' and outputs calibrated probabilities rather than hard binary predictions. Treat the fatal probability as a risk indicator, not a certainty.
