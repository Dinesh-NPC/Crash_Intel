Road Safety Facts

The reality of road accidents

Key statistics that make road safety analytics critical for saving lives.

🚨
1/min
Every Minute Counts
A road accident occurs every minute in India. Over 4,50,000 accidents are reported annually — one of the highest in the world.
⏰
5–7 PM
Peak Danger Window
Evening rush hour consistently records the highest accident count. Driver fatigue after work combined with peak traffic volume creates the most dangerous window.
🌧️
2×
Weather Multiplies Risk
Rain doubles stopping distance. Fog reduces visibility to under 10 meters. Ice makes roads 10× more dangerous than dry conditions.
